How It Works

From question to resolved query.

Every response is grounded in official source documents — the assistant never guesses on matters of policy.

ASK

Citizen asks, in their language

Text or voice, in any of 12+ supported languages, on web, WhatsApp, or a service kiosk.

→
UNDERSTAND

Intent is identified

Natural language processing extracts the department, service, and specific need.

→
RETRIEVE

Official documents consulted

Relevant scheme rules, forms, and policy text are retrieved from verified sources.

→
RESPOND

Grounded answer, with source

A plain-language answer is generated and cited back to its originating document.

→
GUIDE

Next steps, mapped out

Application checklists and required documents are laid out step by step.
